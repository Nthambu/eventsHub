Refund Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2025 · madfuns.com

This Refund Policy sets out the circumstances and procedure for refunds on tickets purchased through Madfuns for events organised, promoted or sponsored by Event Organisers on the Platform. By purchasing a ticket, you agree to this Policy subject to rights that cannot be excluded under Kenyan law.

1. General Rule

All ticket sales are final and non-refundable unless one of the exceptions below applies. Please confirm all event details — date, time, venue, ticket category — before completing your purchase.

2. When a Refund May Be Available

  • Event cancellation — if an event is cancelled and not rescheduled, eligible Ticket Holders may be offered a refund as communicated by the Event Organiser.
  • Material change — a significant change to the date, venue or nature of the event that materially affects your ability to attend.
  • Applicable law — where a refund is required under Kenyan consumer protection law.
  • Discretionary refund — the Event Organiser may authorise a refund in circumstances not expressly covered by this Policy.

3. When a Refund Is Not Available

Refunds will not ordinarily be provided for:

  • change of mind or personal plans;
  • illness, personal emergency, work or family commitments;
  • travel, transport or accommodation difficulties;
  • purchase of the wrong ticket category, date or event;
  • failure to attend, late arrival, or removal for misconduct;
  • tickets lost, stolen, damaged or invalidated by the Ticket Holder;
  • tickets purchased from an unauthorised third party;
  • minor changes to the programme, supporting acts or timings; or
  • claims based solely on personal preference or expectations.

4. Postponed or Rescheduled Events

Where an event is postponed or rescheduled, your ticket will ordinarily remain valid for the new date. If you cannot attend the rescheduled event, a refund will only be available where the Event Organiser expressly makes refunds available or where required by law.

5. Refund Amount & Method

Approved refunds cover the ticket purchase price actually paid. Service and platform fees may be excluded unless required by law. Refunds are processed to the original payment method (M-Pesa or card) within 7–14 business days. Processing times may vary depending on your bank or mobile money operator.

6. How to Request a Refund

Contact us at support@madfuns.com with your order number and reason for the refund request. Requests must be submitted within 48 hours of the qualifying event or notification of cancellation.

7. Contact

Refund queries: support@madfuns.com
